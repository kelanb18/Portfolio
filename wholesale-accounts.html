Wholesale accounts and internal tools

I built account workflows and admin tools around how our wholesale customers do business with us: company creation, customer IDs, pricing access, and Sage account history.

The account workflow

Wholesale accounts need more context than an email address. Staff also need customer numbers, payment terms, purchase history, and the right pricing access. I worked on bringing that information and those workflows into Shopify.

What I built

The initial application and approval flow created a company, assigned a customer number, and granted access. The approach later changed to company creation during checkout, using customer and order webhooks with a returning-customer fallback. I also built an admin customer block showing Sage history and recent orders.

Following up on the exceptions

I backfilled verified Sage customers, corrected terms labels for 832 customers, and restored access for 156 verified wholesale customers who were missing it. I also fixed approval bugs and a bulk-order pricing leak.

What remained open

Existing businesses using new email addresses could still create duplicate companies. I investigated those cases and documented saved-card and draft-order limitations; the work did not resolve every account-reconciliation issue.

Part of a broader effort

This project is part of my ongoing work to modernize Easy USA, connecting improvements to the store with the systems and processes behind it.
