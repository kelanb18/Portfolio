Recurring reporting

Reconciling the sources

Sage BusinessWorks is the source of truth for total company revenue. Shopify supplies online orders, Sage Live.SQL supplies SKU-level line items, and Google Ads and Search Console supply paid and organic channel data. Bringing these together connects the online store with the wider business.

A consistent view of the trend

The reports include 27 weeks of week-over-week history and 24 months of month-over-month history. Both views show total revenue, online revenue, and percentage change, alongside year-to-date totals and same-period prior-year comparisons.

What is selling and who is buying

SKU-level data supports top-50 product rankings by revenue, both overall and for the current week, alongside a top-30 customer ranking. These views give the sales and operations team a way to examine performance beyond the top-line total.

Putting marketing beside sales

Google Ads summaries show spend, impressions, click-through rate, and conversions. Search Console adds organic search traffic, top pages, and device breakdowns. The report puts these signals alongside sales performance without treating them as interchangeable measures.
